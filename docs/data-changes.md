# Data changes: spreadsheet → database

Generated by `python -m cotw import-v3`. Every deviation of `data/countries/` from
the v3 spreadsheet (`cotw v3.csv`), with its source. Later edits are listed by hand
under [Changes after the import](#changes-after-the-import-applied).

The languages `en` and `de` in the rows below (`name.en`, `formal_name.{en,de}`, …) are the
codes from before the deck languages became locales: `en` means `en-US`, `de` means `de-CH`
(`name.en-US`, `formal_name.de-CH`). The rows are kept as written.

## Systematic changes

| What | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Status values | `Souvereign Country` / `Dependency` / `Disputed` | `sovereign` / `dependency` / `disputed` |
| Region names | spreadsheet spelling (`East Africa`, `West Asia`, …) | UN M49 names as in `tags.txt` (`Eastern Africa`, `Western Asia`, …) |
| Country label of dependencies | parent ISO code (`FR`, `GB`, …) | dropped, derivable from `dependency_of` |
| Country label of disputed entries | `disputed` / `umstritten` | dropped, derivable from `status` |
| Long form | `EN Long From` / `DE Langform` | `formal_name.{en,de}`, empty cells omitted |
| Capital labels | free text | `role` (machine-readable) + `label.{en,de}` (display text, unchanged) |
| Borders | ISO-2 codes B1–B14 | COTW ids, recomputed from Natural Earth land adjacency |
| Capitals | names only | + Wikidata QID and coordinates |

## Brazilian Portuguese (PT-BR): adopted names and deviations

The pt-BR locale adopts and source-checks the translation contributed in public GitHub PR #2 and was
checked entry by entry against the *Manual de redação oficial e diplomática do Itamaraty*
(23 July 2026 update) and the accessible FUNAG table *Topônimos e gentílicos* (revision 684,
checked 7 October 2026). The audit covers all 248 entries, every short and formal name, capital,
capital label, Wikipedia link and translated deck string. For the 53 entries whose first audit
lacked field-level fallback provenance, the audit now records the concrete evidence: an available
FUNAG row and Receita Federal's maintained pt-BR territory table first, then the exact ISO entity
record and GNS/GNIS/SCAR place-name record as applicable. Official constitutional or
administering-authority records support the exceptional sourced formal names and capital roles.
Contributor data and this policy document are not treated as independent naming evidence. The EU
list remains only a pt-PT wording cross-check.

After the source-first re-review, COTW uses the Receita fallback form exactly for AX, BQ, BV, CX,
CC, TF, HM, NF, PN, SH, GS, SJ, VI and VG; Guernsey likewise uses the cited local capital form
`St. Peter Port`. These replace contributor forms for which no independent pt-BR evidence was
found. Fifteen self-translated formal names are deliberately absent rather than inferred from an
English or constitutional form: AX, BH, CX, GG, HK, IT, JE, XK, MO, YT, NF, MP, PR, SL and WF.
The field audit records each absence and the sources checked.

The remaining differences from Receita are supported or governed as follows; the field audit
records the exact source value and outcome for every affected field:

- **FUNAG-backed forms:** FO `Ilhas Faroes` → `Ilhas Féroe`; GL `Groelândia` →
  `Groenlândia`; GU drops the tax-system qualifier; IM `Ilha do Homem` → `Ilha de Man`; RE,
  PM, MF and SX use the FUNAG forms; TK and WF omit a redundant leading `Ilhas`.
- **Approved status/entity wording:** FK uses the Manual form `Ilhas Malvinas`; PS uses
  `Palestina` / `Estado da Palestina`; TW uses `República da China (Taiwan)` rather than the
  Receita recognition qualifier. The Taiwan form is attested by the Brazilian Ministry of
  Agriculture's [10 August 2017 agenda](https://www.gov.br/agricultura/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/2017-2018/agenda-do-diretor-do-departamento-de-sanidade-vegetal/2017-08-10),
  checked 8 October 2026. EH combines `Saara Ocidental` with the sourced `República Saaraui`
  alternative. XK has no Receita/ISO entry; its unsourced translated formal name is omitted.

## Natural Earth reconciliation

Source: Natural Earth ne_10m_admin_0_map_units v5.1.1 (https://naciscdn.org/naturalearth/10m/cultural/ne_10m_admin_0_map_units.zip), public domain. Map units without an ISO code were folded into the entry that
controls them de facto:

- `CNM` → CY: UN buffer zone in Cyprus
- `CYN` → CY: Northern Cyprus: de facto separate, but not an ISO entity; folded into Cyprus
- `ESB` → GB: Dhekelia sovereign base area: UK territory on the island of Cyprus
- `KAS` → IN: Siachen Glacier: Indian-administered, de facto rule
- `KNX` → KR: Korean DMZ, southern half
- `KNZ` → KP: Korean DMZ, northern half
- `SOL` → SO: Somaliland: de facto separate, but not an ISO entity; folded into Somalia
- `SYU` → SY: UNDOF zone on the Golan Heights, Syrian side
- `USG` → US: Guantanamo Bay naval base: US-controlled under lease, de facto rule
- `WSB` → GB: Akrotiri sovereign base area: UK territory on the island of Cyprus

Ignored map units (no entry of their own, no effect on any border): Antarctica (ATA), Bajo Nuevo Bank (Petrel Islands) (BJN), Bir Tawil (BRT), Paracel Islands (PFA), Scarborough Reef (SCR), Serranilla Bank (SER), Southern Patagonian Ice Field (SPI), Spratly Islands (PGA).

Manual overrides of the computed adjacency (`data/overrides/borders.yaml`):

- add BW–ZM: Botswana and Zambia share a ~150 m land border at the Kazungula quadripoint (border agreement 2013, Kazungula Bridge 2021); Natural Earth draws a Namibia–Zimbabwe contact there instead.
- add CN–MO: Macao borders Zhuhai (Portas do Cerco / Cotai) by land; Natural Earth 10m draws Macao without a shared boundary line. The spreadsheet lists CN for MO, and the issue asks for symmetry with HK/MO.
- add CA–GL: Since the 2022 Canada–Denmark agreement Hans Island is split between Canada and Greenland, creating a land border; Natural Earth 5.1.1 still assigns the whole island to one side.
